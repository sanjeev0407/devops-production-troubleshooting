# Production Incident Ticket
**Short description:** `[P#] [ENV] [SERVICE] — symptom`

## Business impact
Affected users/tenants, transaction, scope and workaround.

## Details
Incident ID, priority, environment, account alias, region, start time/timezone, detection source, resource and redacted error/correlation ID.

## Investigation (5W+1H)
What / where / when / who / why (facts vs hypothesis) / how to reproduce.

## Actions
| Time | Action | Result | Engineer |
|---|---|---|---|

## Escalation
Team/contact, reason, evidence and specific assistance required.

## Communication
Initial update, latest update and next update due.

## Resolution
Mitigation, validation, observation window, final status and RCA link.
