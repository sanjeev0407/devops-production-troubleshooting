# Root Cause Analysis Template

## Summary
Incident ID, service/environment/region, priority, start/detection/mitigation/recovery/resolution times (timezone), business impact and duration.

## Executive summary
3–5 factual sentences: what failed, who was affected, mitigation and current status.

## Five Whys
1. Why did customer see symptom?
2. Why did technical condition occur?
3. Why was it possible?
4. Why did controls not prevent/detect it?
5. What systemic change prevents recurrence?
